# Giovanni chapter sprite atlas

Mode: built-in image generation; new transparent raster atlas (stylized-concept).
Saved source: `assets/dogs/giovanni-source.png`.
Consumers: Dalmatian and Belgian Laekenois front/back PNGs, cropped and encoded by
`tools/dogs/convert_sprites.py` to the established four-shade Game Boy format.

Exact prompt:

Create a transparent PNG sprite atlas for a Game Boy dog RPG. Exactly four equally sized square cells in one horizontal row, aligned centers and generous transparent gutters, no text or borders. Cell 1: front battle sprite of an adult Dalmatian dog facing viewer slightly left, entire body four legs, white coat with distinct black spots, floppy black-and-white ears, athletic friendly alert stance. Cell 2: back battle sprite of the same Dalmatian, viewed from behind facing upper right, head and shoulders plus back silhouette. Cell 3: front battle sprite of a majestic Belgian Laekenois dog, rough wiry fawn coat with dark muzzle, pointed erect ears, entire four-legged dog body, alert storm guardian stance, no wings, no accessories, breed recognizable. Cell 4: back battle sprite of the same Belgian Laekenois viewed from behind facing upper right, erect ears and wiry shoulders. Authentic crisp 1990s Game Boy pixel art, strictly four grayscale colors black #000000, dark gray #555555, light gray #AAAAAA, white #FFFFFF, transparent background. Front sprites should fit approximately 56 by 56 logical pixels, back sprites 32 by 32, scaled up cleanly without smoothing. Keep every sprite separate within its own quarter, no decorative lightning or scenery, no shadows outside body.
